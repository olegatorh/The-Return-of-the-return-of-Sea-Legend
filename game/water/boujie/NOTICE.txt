Used here: foam.png (= shader/foam_2.png), ocean_albedo.png (= shader/ocean_albedo_white_highcontrast.png) and the ideas of water.gdshader
(Gerstner crest foam, shore foam from the depth texture, Beer's-law depth fog between shallow and deep colour, screen-texture refraction, Fresnel).
sea_boujie.gdshader is a rewrite for this port: the wave shape comes from SeaWaves.cs (the same harmonics the ships ride on), the colours,
fog, sky reflection and bump map come from the game's weather scripts. Selected by settings.ini [game] WaterShader = boujie | classic.
